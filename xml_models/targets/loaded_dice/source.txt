Assets are copies of mjcf/loaded_dice/ (kept unchanged for reference):
  assets/object_0.obj            <- mjcf/loaded_dice/loaded_dice_0.obj            (ABS case, red)
  assets/object_1.obj            <- mjcf/loaded_dice/loaded_dice_1.obj            (brass insert)
  assets/object_collision_0.obj  <- mjcf/loaded_dice/loaded_dice_collision_0.obj  (convex hull)
  assets/material.mtl            <- mjcf/loaded_dice.mtl
Meshes are in millimetres, so object.xml scales them by 0.001.

object_cad_gt.csv, row 2 (the row the simulator reads):
  total_mass, cx..cz and ixx..izx are taken from the <inertial> element of
  mjcf/loaded_dice/loaded_dice_gt_inertial.xml, i.e. the voxel-measured ground truth
  (gt_mass_distr.csv at lod=7). MuJoCo's fullinertia order (ixx iyy izz ixy ixz iyz)
  is remapped to this file's order (ixx iyy izz ixy iyz izx).
  pixx..pizz and rx..rz are the eigen-decomposition of that tensor; the column order
  reproduces the diaginertia MuJoCo computes for loaded_dice_gt_inertial.xml.
  aabb_scale = 0.0345 m is max(|vertex coordinate|) over the two visual meshes, the
  same convention as the other targets (collision geoms are group 3 and not rendered).

object_cad_gt.csv, rows 3+ (component block, not read by the current pipeline):
  masses computed by MuJoCo from mjcf/loaded_dice/loaded_dice_density.xml (catalog
  densities, exact signed mesh volumes). They sum to 0.34026 kg, 2.7% above the voxel
  total_mass above, which is the discretisation bias documented in
  loaded_dice_gt_inertial.xml.

Not provided: ground_truth.csv (per-voxel mass distribution). main.py only warns when
it is absent.

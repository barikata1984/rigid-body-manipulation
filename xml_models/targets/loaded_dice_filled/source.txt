The simulator assets were generated from:
  ../loaded dice filled/loaded dice.obj
  ../loaded dice filled/loaded dice.mtl

Conversion command (obj2mjcf 0.0.25):
  obj2mjcf --obj-dir <staging-dir> --obj-filter '^loaded_dice_filled\.obj$' --save-mjcf --compile-model --decompose --overwrite

The OBJ's mtllib entry was normalized from "loaded dice.mtl" to
"loaded_dice_filled.mtl" in the staging copy because obj2mjcf treats an
unquoted space as the end of the material-library path. The source OBJ and MTL
were not modified.

object_cad_gt.csv is generated from every row of:
  ../loaded dice filled/gt_mass_distr.csv

The legacy filename object_cad_gt.csv is retained because the simulator loader
requires it; its values are voxel-derived rather than CAD mass properties.
